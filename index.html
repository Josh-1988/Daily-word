About
The word with Joshua Kakaire — a daily devotional for 2026. Each day brings a verse, a reflection, a confession and further reading. Devotions unlock on their date; today’s is always on the home screen.
Sharing
Devotions may be shared in full and unchanged, with the author’s name attached. Copying, editing and downloading are switched off for readers.
Install on your phone
Open this file in your browser, then choose “Add to Home screen” from the browser menu. It will open like an app.
